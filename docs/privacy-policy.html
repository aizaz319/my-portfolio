Privacy Policy

SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt
Effective date: November 24, 2026
Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt (also shown on device as “SnapQuest AI”; the “App”), published by Aizaz Ali Shah (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the App on Android via Google Play.

By using SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who this App is for

SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

2. Summary

  • The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob (interstitial and optional rewarded ads) and may use the device Advertising ID.
  • Camera frames are processed on your device for gameplay and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Local game progress (scores, XP, achievements, settings) is stored on your device.
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in may send your Play Games identity and scores to Google for online leaderboards.
  • We do not create SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt user accounts. Core gameplay works without signing in.

3. Information we process

3.1 Information processed only on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device (for example in app preferences):

  • Gameplay stats such as high scores, match counts, accuracy, XP, level, and unlocked achievements
  • Daily challenge best score and related local progress
  • In-app settings such as selected difficulty and content pack

This local data is used only to operate the App on your device. It is not transmitted by us to our own servers (we do not operate a SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt backend that stores your profile).

3.2 Camera

SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt requests camera permission so you can find real-world objects during gameplay. Camera frames are analyzed on-device using the App’s object-detection model. We do not save photos to your gallery for this purpose, and we do not upload camera images or video to our servers. Ads are not shown during the live camera hunt.

3.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads, including:

  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown at natural pauses (for example after matches)
  • Rewarded ads — optional ads you choose to watch for in-game benefits (such as bonus XP or bonus hunt chances)

To serve, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • Device and app identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and general device / network information
  • Ad interaction and performance data (for example impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS collected by the App for ads)

This advertising data is processed by Google (and partners Google works with), not on a SnapQuest backend operated by us. How ads are personalized may depend on your device settings, regional requirements, and Google’s policies. You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings (for example Ads / Privacy settings) and via options Google provides.

For more detail, see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy information.

3.4 Optional Google Play Games Services

If you choose to use online leaderboards, you may sign in with your existing Google Play Games account. The App does not create its own accounts and does not require Play Games sign-in for core gameplay.

When you use Play Games features, Google may process:

  • User IDs / gamer identity (such as Play Games player ID, gamertag, and avatar), for account management and leaderboard display
  • Gameplay activity such as scores you submit to leaderboards, for App functionality
  • Diagnostics and related performance data collected by the Play Games Services SDK to help Google maintain and improve the service (analytics / diagnostics)

Leaderboard and profile visibility follow your Google Play Games privacy settings (for example friends-only, public, or private). This data is handled by Google as part of Play Games Services. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and your Play Games profile settings.

3.5 Score sharing (optional, user-initiated)

If you use the share feature, the App opens your device’s share sheet with a text summary of your score. You choose where to send it. We do not control third-party apps you share to.

3.6 Information we do not collect in this version

  • We do not collect precise GPS location for the App
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS, call logs, or files for gameplay
  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not operate our own user-account or advertising profile database for SnapQuest: AI Object Hunt

4. How we use information

Information described above is used to:

  • Provide App functionality (gameplay, local progress, optional leaderboards)
  • Support optional account-related Play Games features (sign-in identity)
  • Allow Google Play Games Services to operate, including SDK diagnostics/analytics for service quality
  • Show ads through Google AdMob and support optional rewarded in-game benefits

Advertising-related processing (including use of the Advertising ID where applicable) is performed primarily by Google AdMob and its partners according to their policies and your applicable privacy choices.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information.

When you use optional Play Games features, relevant data (such as gamer identity and scores) is shared with Google and may be visible to other players according to your Play Games privacy settings.

When ads are requested or shown, advertising-related identifiers and device/network information may be shared with Google AdMob and advertising partners that Google works with, so ads can be served and measured.

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: Local stats and settings remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.
  • Play Games data: Data associated with Google Play Games (including leaderboard-related data) is managed by Google. You can review and delete Play Games–related data through your Play Games profile and/or Google Account settings.
  • Advertising data: Ad-related data retained by Google is managed under Google’s privacy practices. You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android system settings.

To request help related to privacy or data handled by the App, contact us using the email below. Because we do not operate our own user-account database, we cannot delete Google account, Play Games, or AdMob advertising data on Google’s behalf; those requests must be handled through Google’s tools and your device settings.
